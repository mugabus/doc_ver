About Us

Read more

The ICGLR Regional Certification Mechanism was established to promote responsible mineral trade in the Great Lakes Region. Our comprehensive framework ensures transparency, traceability, and compliance with international standards throughout the mineral supply chain. By implementing rigorous auditing procedures and certification protocols, we work to eliminate conflict minerals and promote ethical mining practices that protect both workers and communities across member states.

Member States

Read more

The International Conference on the Great Lakes Region (ICGLR) comprises 12 member states committed to regional cooperation and responsible mineral trade. These nations work together to implement the Regional Certification Mechanism, ensuring conflict-free minerals and promoting ethical mining practices throughout the Great Lakes Region. Through harmonized standards and collaborative efforts, member states contribute to sustainable development and regional stability.

Each member state maintains its own certification database that synchronizes with the central ICGLR regional database, enabling real-time tracking of mineral flows across borders. This integrated approach ensures full traceability from mine site to export, supporting international due diligence requirements and promoting transparency in the mineral supply chain.
